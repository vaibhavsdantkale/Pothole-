How PotholeAI Works

From citizen upload to AI severity scoring and municipal repair tracking in four seamless steps.

1. Capture & Tag

Citizen takes a road photo. GPS automatically retrieves the exact coordinates and street landmark.

2. YOLO Vision AI

Python FastAPI runs pre-trained YOLO inference to locate bounding boxes and calculate confidence scores.

3. Severity Scoring

Image size-based heuristic engine rates damage as Low, Medium, or High danger.

4. Telegram & Action

Java Spring Boot dispatches real-time Telegram alerts to maintenance teams for rapid repair dispatch.
